🔒 User Privacy & Transparency

Privacy Policy

InstaFrameStudio is committed to protecting user privacy and maintaining transparency regarding how our Android applications handle data and permissions.

Last Updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InstaFrameStudio ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android applications available on the Google Play Store, including PixLoom Photo Frames.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information unless voluntarily provided by the user through email or support communication.

Non-Personal Information

Our applications and integrated third-party services may collect certain non-personal information, including:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Country and language preferences
  • General app usage statistics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information

Photos & Media Access

Our photo editing applications may request access to device photos, media, or storage solely for editing and creating photo frame designs selected by the user.

We do not upload, store, share, or transmit user photos to our servers.

Advertising Services

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within our applications. AdMob may collect and process certain device identifiers and usage data to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

Learn more about how Google uses data:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Analytics

We may use analytics services such as Firebase Analytics to better understand app performance, feature usage, and overall user experience in order to improve our applications.

Permissions

Depending on app functionality, our applications may request permissions including:

  • Photos / Media / File Access
  • Storage Access
  • Internet Access

These permissions are used only for core app functionality and improving the user experience.

Children’s Privacy

Our applications are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices designed to protect user information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Our applications may integrate third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with the updated revision date.
